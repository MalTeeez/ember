# Ember 1.3

- No hover highlight and no tooltips while another window (for example a game) covers the widget.
- Usage tooltip shows the pace in use (%/h or %/day). While Ember has too few own samples, it shows when the recent pace starts. The weekly recent pace now needs 6 h of samples (was 12 h). Per-model limits also use a recent pace.
- A GPU process line shows only under its own GPU line, and only while that line shows.
- Kept space that is near its end is released together with other size changes, so the widget moves less often.
